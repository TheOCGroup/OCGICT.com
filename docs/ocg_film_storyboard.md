# OCG Flagship Brand Film — Preproduction Storyboard & Shot List

## Project Title: *Built Smarter: The Architecture of an OCG Acquisition*
- **Primary Master**: 90 seconds (1:30) | 4K UHD (16:9 / 2.39:1 Anamorphic framing) | 24fps
- **Derivative Formats**:
  * **9:16 Vertical Reel / TikTok**: Center-cut framing with dynamic typography for mobile social channels.
  * **1:1 Square Ad Cut**: 30-second condensed cut for LinkedIn / Instagram feeds.

## 2. Technical Production Specifications

### Wichita B-Roll Locations
1. **Historic College Hill**: S Rutan Ave, Broadview Ave, Douglas Ave commercial corridor.
2. **Crown Heights**: Glendale Ave brick ranches and mature shade trees.
3. **Downtown / Delano**: West Douglas Clock Tower and historic worker cottages.
4. **Closing Table**: Title company conference room (Kansas Title / Security 1st Title Wichita).

### Soundtrack & Sound Design Direction
- **Music**: Cinematic neo-classical score building into modern hybrid percussion (think Max Richter meets modern architectural documentary).
- **Sound Design**: Tangible foley (lockbox click, heavy door creak, blueprint unroll, closing pen signature) blended with subtle digital data chimes.
