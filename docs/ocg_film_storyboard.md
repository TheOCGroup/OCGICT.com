# OCG Flagship Brand Film — Preproduction Storyboard & Shot List

## Project Title: *Built Smarter: The Architecture of an OCG Acquisition*
- **Duration**: 90 seconds (1:30)
- **Format**: 4K Cinematic (2.39:1 Anamorphic), 24fps
- **Tone**: Cinematic, institutional, grounded, fast-paced yet meticulous. No cheesy AI holograms or robotic tropes. Real architectural texture and authentic Wichita atmosphere.
